   <h1>Vexyra</h1>

**Vexyra** is a open-source guide for Windows 10 and 11 users who want to get the most out of their PC. It provides proven methods, programs, and tips for achieving the highest possible gaming performance by optimizing the System, BIOS, and other settings. Vexyra will show you how to do it correctly and how to monitor your hardware for performance.

## 🛠️Tech Stack
- **Framework:** [Nextjs](https://nextjs.org/) and [Nextra.site](https://nextra.site/)
- **Styles:** [Tailwindcss](https://tailwindcss.com/)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Vexyra is a new and emerging project, we are working on writing guides and trusting us.
